# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Respect different experience levels and environments.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Share enough technical detail to help others reproduce issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the repository issue tracker or another published project contact channel.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
